1. About Dash

Dash ("Dash", "we") is a free, non-commercial student project that analyses a VALORANT player's own finished matches. By using Dash you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use Dash.

2. Who can use Dash

You must be at least 13 years old, or older if your country sets a higher age for using online services without a parent's consent. You must have a VALORANT account that you are allowed to use.

3. Your account and your data

  • You can only load data from your own account. Do not use Dash to look up, profile or scout other players.
  • Dash only analyses matches you choose to load, and only after you opt in to sharing your own data. You can stop at any time by signing out and revoking Dash's access in your Riot account settings.
  • How we handle data is described in the Privacy Policy.

4. What you may not do

  • Use Dash to gain an unfair advantage, to read hidden information about a match in progress, or to replace any in-game ranking or queue.
  • Try to find out who the other (anonymized) players in a match are, or collect data about them.
  • Copy, resell or republish data from Dash, or use it to build profiles of players.
  • Break the Riot Games API rules, overload the service, or try to get around any limit or security measure.
  • Use Dash for anything unlawful.

5. Not medical, coaching or competitive advice

Dash gives simple statistics and automatic feedback made by rules and machine learning. The feedback can be wrong, and it is based on a small number of rounds. It is for learning only. Do not rely on it for decisions about competitive play, money or anything important.

6. Prototype and availability

Dash is a prototype. It is provided "as is" and "as available", without promises that it is accurate, always working, or free of errors. We may change, pause or stop Dash at any time, including if Riot Games changes or removes API access.
