Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

The short version

  • Dash looks at your own VALORANT matches, after you opt in.
  • Other players in your matches are replaced by p1, p2 ... right away. Their names and IDs are not saved or shown.
  • Your match data is processed in your browser. We do not keep a database of player data.
  • We do not sell data, show ads or share data with third parties.

1. Who we are

Dash is a free student project. For privacy questions, contact: [your contact email].

2. What data Dash handles

Your own match data
From Riot's VALORANT match API, for matches you load: kills, deaths, assists, damage, shots that hit, weapon bought, credits, round results and, if the map feature is added, positions of your own kills and deaths.
Your sign-in
When sign-in is available it uses Riot Sign On. Dash receives only what Riot returns for the permissions you approve, and never your Riot password.
Other players in your matches
Riot's match data contains other players. Dash replaces them with p1, p2 ... when it converts the data. Their names, Riot IDs, PUUIDs and the match ID are not saved or shown, and Dash does not show their stats as a profile.
Settings on your device
Your browser's local storage keeps small preferences, such as which table columns you chose and the examples you teach the good / not good rating. This stays on your device and you can clear it in your browser settings.

3. How and why we use it

  • To show you statistics and feedback about your own play (our only purpose).
  • To run the playstyle grouping and weapon prediction, which run in your browser on the data you loaded.
  • We do not use the data for advertising, ranking players, scouting, matchmaking or to give an advantage during a match.

4. Where data is kept and for how long

Match data is held in your browser while the page is open, and in any file you choose to download from Dash. We do not store it on a server of our own. Settings in local storage stay until you clear them. The website is hosted by a third-party service, which may keep normal technical logs of visits (such as time and approximate location of the connection). We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data or give it to anyone. We share data with Riot Games only in the way the product needs: requests to its API to get your own matches. We may share information if the law requires it.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Stop at any time by signing out and removing Dash's access in your Riot account settings.
  • Delete what is on your device by clearing your browser's site data for Dash and deleting any files you downloaded.
  • Because we keep no central copy of your data, there is nothing else for us to delete. If you think we hold something about you, write to [your contact email] and we will check and remove it.
  • Depending on where you live you may have rights to see, correct or delete your data and to complain to a privacy authority.

7. Children

Dash is not for children under 13 (or the higher age your country sets). We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used Dash, contact us and we will remove any related information.

8. Security

Data is processed in your browser, which keeps it off our systems, and Dash only talks to the services it needs. No method is completely secure, so keep your Riot account secure as well.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If we change what data we use, we will ask you again before using it.
